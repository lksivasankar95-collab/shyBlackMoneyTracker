## 4. Privacy Policy

### Core Content

**ShyBlack Finance Privacy Policy**

*Effective: July 1, 2026*

1. **Data Storage**: All financial data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal financial information on remote servers.

2. **No Account Required**: ShyBlack Finance operates without user accounts. No email, phone number, or personal identifier is required.

3. **Local Processing**: All calculations, analytics, and financial insights are computed entirely on-device.

4. **No Tracking**: We do not use analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or tracking cookies. No third-party services receive your data.

5. **Backup Data**: If you choose to export backup files, these remain under your control. We cannot access them.

6. **Permissions**: The app requests storage access only for document upload and PDF export. These are used solely at your direction.

7. **Third-Party Services**: The app uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or data processing services.

8. **Changes**: This policy may be updated. Significant changes will be notified in-app.

9. **Contact**: For privacy concerns, contact the developer via the Google Play Store listing.

## 6. App Description (Full)

**Title**: ShyBlack Finance - Smart Money Tracker

**Description**:

Take control of your finances with ShyBlack Finance - a privacy-first, offline personal finance manager designed for people who value both functionality and aesthetics.

**TRACK EVERYTHING**
- Income & expenses with detailed categories
- Multi-account management (cash, bank, credit cards)
- Recurring transactions with auto-execution
- EMI tracking with intelligent payment schedules
- Borrow/Lend ledger with balance tracking

**SMART INSIGHTS**
- Financial health score with personalized recommendations
- Net worth tracking with historical trends
- Cash flow forecasting and projections
- Category trends and spending patterns
- Monthly and annual PDF reports

**DASHBOARD AT A GLANCE**
- Real-time net worth and balance
- Monthly income vs expense comparison
- Pending EMIs, recurring transactions, and bills
- Recent activity with quick edit/delete
- Financial goals with progress tracking

**DOCUMENT VAULT**
- Store receipts, statements, and financial documents
- PDF preview and sharing
- Organized document management

**PRIVACY FIRST**
- 100% offline - no servers, no accounts, no tracking
- PIN lock protection
- Complete data ownership
- No permissions required beyond storage

**DARK MODE BY DESIGN**
- Beautiful matte dark interface
- Neon green accents
- Glass morphism UI elements
- Smooth animations and transitions

ShyBlack Finance is completely free with no ads, no subscriptions, and no hidden costs.
